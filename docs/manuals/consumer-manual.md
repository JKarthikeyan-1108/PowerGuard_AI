# Consumer Manual

Welcome to the PowerGuard Consumer Portal. We put control of your electricity usage back in your hands.

## Getting Started
1. **Progressive Web App (PWA):** You can install this application directly to your mobile phone's home screen. Open the site in Chrome or Safari and select "Add to Home Screen".
2. **Biometric Login:** Navigate to Settings to enable WebAuthn. This allows you to log in securely using your phone's fingerprint or FaceID scanner without remembering a password.

## Managing Your Energy
1. **Live Usage Dashboard:** Track your real-time electricity consumption (kWh) and active load.
2. **Dynamic Tariffs:** Your cost per kWh changes based on grid demand. The dashboard displays the current rate (e.g., $0.15/kWh vs Peak $0.25/kWh). Shift heavy appliance usage to Off-Peak hours to save money!
3. **Bill Prediction:** Our AI models predict your end-of-month bill based on current habits.
4. **AI Energy Assistant:** Have a question about your bill? Click the Chat icon in the bottom right to talk to your personal Energy Copilot. It knows your recent usage and can offer specific advice to lower your costs.
