# Utility Officer Manual

Welcome to the PowerGuard Field Operations Portal.

## Field Operations
1. **Incident Triage:** The dashboard highlights high-priority anomalies (e.g., severe voltage drops or bypassed meters).
2. **Smart Grid Digital Twin:**
   - Navigate to the `Digital Twin` map.
   - This provides a live, topological view of your substations, transformers, and meters.
   - Colors indicate load stress (Red = Overloaded Transformer).
   - Trace power flow visually to identify the exact point of failure or theft.
3. **Predictive Maintenance:** View the "Remaining Useful Life" of transformers. Schedule maintenance *before* a catastrophic failure causes a neighborhood blackout.
4. **AI Copilot:** While in the field, use the AI Copilot's Voice-to-Text capability. Tap the microphone and ask, "What is the status of Transformer T-101?" to get hands-free diagnostics while you work.
