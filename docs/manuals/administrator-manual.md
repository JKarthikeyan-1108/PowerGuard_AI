# Administrator Manual

Welcome to PowerGuard v2.0. As an Administrator, you have complete control over your Organization's grid.

## Core Capabilities
1. **Grid Monitoring:** View real-time aggregated metrics (Total Active Load, Average Voltage) on the main dashboard.
2. **Incidents & Alerts:** View theft predictions and grid instability alerts. You can assign these alerts to Utility Officers for field investigation.
3. **Billing & Tariffs:** Define dynamic tariffs (Peak vs Off-Peak rates) to incentivize grid load balancing.
4. **API Integrations:** 
   - Navigate to `/admin/integrations`.
   - You can upload `.csv` or `.xlsx` files to bulk-register thousands of smart meters.
   - You can export active incidents to CSV for import into Power BI.
5. **AI Copilot:** 
   - Click the Chat Icon in the bottom right.
   - Ask the AI to summarize active incidents, explain predictions, or give a high-level grid report. The AI is automatically context-aware of your administrative role.
