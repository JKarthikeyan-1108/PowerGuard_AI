# Consumer User Manual

Welcome to the PowerGuard Consumer Portal. This dashboard empowers you to monitor your energy usage, save money, and contribute to a greener grid.

## 1. Live Monitoring
- **Navigation:** Click on `Live Monitoring`.
- **Function:** Watch your electricity consumption in real-time. The dashboard updates every few seconds to show your current Power (kW), Voltage, and Current.
- **Tip:** Turn on a heavy appliance (like an AC or microwave) and watch the graph spike to understand its energy impact.

## 2. Usage History
- **Navigation:** Click on `Usage History`.
- **Function:** View your historical daily, weekly, and monthly consumption. Compare your usage against neighborhood averages to see how efficient your household is.

## 3. Bill Prediction
- **Navigation:** Click on `Bill Prediction`.
- **Function:** Our AI analyzes your usage patterns in the first few days of the month to predict your final monthly bill.
- **Usage:** Use this feature to adjust your habits early in the month if your projected bill exceeds your budget.

## 4. Energy Recommendations
- **Navigation:** Click on `Recommendations`.
- **Function:** Receive personalized, AI-driven tips based on your specific consumption patterns (e.g., "Shift washing machine usage to off-peak hours to save $15/month").
- **Action:** Implement these recommendations to lower your carbon footprint and reduce costs.
